About Beyond Senior Living

A small, intimate assisted living home in Woodbury, Minnesota that cares for residents like family, led by an experienced owner, an RN and a dedicated team of caregivers.

Assisted living

At Beyond Senior Living, every care plan is developed together with our residents, their family members and their medical professionals. We value transparency, so your loved one’s needs are understood and attended to more effectively than they could be at a larger community or in a private home setting.

Our assisted living home gives seniors a safe, comfortable place to live with the right amount of help. Residents keep as much independence as they can, while our staff takes care of the things that have become difficult, from daily personal care to medications, meals and housekeeping.

  • Individualized care plans reviewed with families
  • Awake staff on site 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • RN site visits, with a registered nurse on call 24/7

A trusted and experienced senior care provider

Beyond Senior Living was built on a simple idea: older adults deserve to be cared for the way we would want our own parents cared for. Our owner, registered nurse and caregivers bring real experience in senior care, along with the same warmth to every resident.

As an assisted living home rather than a large facility, we can offer the kind of one-on-one attention that’s hard to find elsewhere. Our staff knows each resident’s routines, preferences and stories.
