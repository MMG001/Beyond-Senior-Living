Homemaker companion

Companionship is the key to a trusted relationship with our caregivers. Our homemaker companions keep the home safe, clean and comfortable, prepare meals, and give residents one-on-one attention that larger settings can’t match.

Meals and snacks

  • Three wholesome, home-cooked meals daily, with special dietary needs provided
  • Monitored food and fluid intake
  • Resident-specific snacks

Housekeeping

A clean, comfortable home helps residents live with more confidence, security and peace of mind. Housekeeping includes, but isn’t limited to, vacuuming, dusting, sweeping and mopping, washing dishes, changing bed linens, laundry and cleaning bathrooms.

Meal preparation and shopping

Our homemakers prepare meals and snacks that follow each resident’s care plan and dietary instructions, and handle grocery shopping for the home.

Programs and activities

Activity plans are tailored to each resident’s needs and abilities, and many are built around familiar household tasks.

  • Games and entertainment: board games, bingo, checkers, trivia and movie nights
  • Spiritual and reflective time: daily reflections, prayer before dinner or bed, and spiritual readings
  • Emotional support: hugs, hand-holding, positive interaction and encouragement from staff

Companionship

Our staff joins residents on walks and trips to social and recreational events, helps with hobbies, and makes time for good conversation every day.
