Personal care assistant (PCA)

Our personal care assistants help residents who aren’t getting around as well as they once did, from daily routines to mobility and comfort, all as part of an established care plan.

Personal care

  • Hygiene assistance with bathing, dressing and grooming
  • Toileting and incontinence care
  • Transfers from bed to chair and other locations

Specialized services

We build relationships and trust with residents who need more specialized care. Services available include:

  • Customized skilled nursing, such as wound care
  • Insulin management and blood glucose monitoring
  • Catheter care
  • One-on-one feeding assistance
  • Escorts to social and medical appointments
  • Incontinence and medical supplies
  • End-of-life hospice care, coordinated on site
  • Other customized services by request

Help around the house

Residents never have to worry that the essentials will be neglected. Our staff helps with laundry, sweeping, dusting, making beds, preparing meals, grocery shopping and running errands when needed.
