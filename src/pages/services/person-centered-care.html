Person-centered care

Beyond Senior Living uses a healthcare approach that organizes treatment around an individual’s values, needs, preferences, and life circumstances, making the person an equal partner in planning and decision-making. It focuses on “what matters to the person,” not just “what is the matter.”

We deliver care that’s personalized around your loved one and you, as well. You won’t have to stress about their safety or comfort and instead can simply focus on spending quality time, together. It’s a better way to live and why so many families across the Twin Cities area count on us for quality care.

Assisted living services

We offer 24/7 support, and our individualized care plan will meet your specific needs with our high quality care.

  • Resident-specific care plans
  • RN site visits, as well as on call 24/7
  • Nurse care coordination, clinical record keeping, and coordination of lab and physician requests
  • Awake staff on site 24/7
  • Excellent staffing ratios
  • 24 hour assistance with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) including but not limited to toileting, bathing, grooming, transferring, feeding, dressing and continence care
  • Medication setup, management, storage and administration
  • Daily cueing and redirection as needed
  • Weekly wellness email updates to families, with weekly vital sign and weight monitoring
  • Socialization groups, exercise and outings
  • Skilled services, plus end-of-life and hospice care
  • Respite care (call for availability)

Types of residents we serve

We care for residents living with:

  • Parkinson’s
  • COPD
  • Congestive heart failure (CHF)
  • Hospice or palliative care
  • Stroke
  • Diabetes, blood glucose monitoring and insulin
  • Mental illness and behavioral challenges

Our staff is also experienced with:

  • Catheters
  • CPAP machines
  • Oxygen
  • TED stockings
  • Nebulizers

Non-medical and instrumental activities of daily living (IADLs)

We offer assistance with:

  • Laundry
  • Sweeping and vacuuming
  • Dusting
  • Making beds
  • Meal preparation
  • Grocery shopping
  • Running errands when needed
  • Making phone calls
  • Managing money when appropriate
  • Transportation
  • Communication devices
  • Going to the doctor
