Health care

Beyond Senior Living provides a complete range of assisted living services, with staff on site 24/7. Having caregivers they can trust helps older adults and adults with disabilities manage daily life and enjoy a better quality of life.

Our approach to care

We believe every resident deserves to feel at home. That’s why we provide needs-based care and health services in a small, comfortable assisted living setting rather than a large institution. The quality of that care starts with the people who give it: we hold our staff to high hiring standards and invest in ongoing training and professionalism.

Safety, security and a comfortable home

  • 24-hour monitored security and fire systems
  • Smoke detectors and fire extinguishers throughout the home
  • Fenced, secured outdoor spaces
  • Fully furnished common areas
  • Family visits welcome

Trained, carefully screened staff

Our caregivers are professionally trained, with clearly defined responsibilities and qualifications. Every staff member completes a thorough background check, including criminal history, before they are hired.

Family members can’t always be there, especially when they’re raising children of their own or live far away. Our staff offers the companionship that helps residents keep their sense of self-worth and dignity every day.

Everyday wellness support

  • Accompanying residents to medical appointments (additional fee)
  • Exercise as prescribed by a doctor
  • Following a prescribed diet
  • Games and hobbies
  • Reading together
  • Meaningful conversation
