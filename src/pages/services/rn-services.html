RN services

Aging comes with real challenges, but the right support makes life easier. Our registered nurse services are customized to each resident’s needs and preferences, so they can live as independently as possible.

Our staff are patient advocates with a strong reputation for quality, compassionate care. Every resident’s plan is designed to strike the right balance between help and independence, and it changes as their needs change.

What our RN services include

  • Resident-specific care plans
  • RN site visits, as well as a registered nurse on call 24/7
  • Nurse care coordination
  • Nurse maintenance of clinical records
  • Nurse coordination of laboratory and physician requests
  • Medication management, storage and administration

Why it matters

Reduced mobility, memory changes, general weakness and chronic conditions can all call for consistent, skilled oversight. Many seniors want that level of care without the feel of a nursing home. Our assisted living home offers both: nursing support around the clock in a comfortable, familiar setting where residents keep their routines and relationships.

How our caregivers support the nursing plan

  • Medication reminders
  • Meal planning and food preparation
  • Light housekeeping
  • Rides to doctor checkups
  • Running errands
  • Basic personal care
  • Joining residents in recreational activities
  • Personalized one-on-one attention
