A safe, loving home for your loved one

Let your loved one enjoy their golden years in a fun, safe and loving environment. Our assisted living home is a place to receive continuous care and still live a full life. Meals and activities are tailored to each resident’s needs and preferences, and families are welcome to visit often.

We have a passion for meeting the needs of seniors in a way that offers both dignity and camaraderie. Residents are enabled, not hindered. Assisted living here means freedom from the things that have become hard to manage, so day-to-day concerns are no longer a burden.

  • Care plans built with residents, families and medical professionals
  • Three home-cooked meals a day, with special diets accommodated
  • Socialization groups, exercise and outings
