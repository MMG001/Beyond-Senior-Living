Accepted forms of payment

Paying for assisted living can feel complicated. We accept several forms of payment and are happy to talk through your options with you.

Private pay

Pay for your loved one’s care directly. We’ll walk you through what their individualized care plan includes.

Long-term care insurance

We accept long-term care insurance. Call us with your policy details and we’ll go over it together.

Medicaid waivers

We accept these Minnesota Medicaid waiver programs:

  • Brain Injury (BI) waiver
  • Community Access for Disability Inclusion (CADI) waiver
  • Elderly Waiver (EW)

Waiver eligibility is determined by your county or tribal nation. If you’re not sure which program fits, give us a call and we’ll help you figure out where to start.
