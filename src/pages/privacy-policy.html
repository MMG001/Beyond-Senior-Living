Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Beyond Senior Living (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through beyondseniorlivingmn.com and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you use our contact form or job application, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, details about your loved one’s care needs, or your employment history. Our online forms are provided by Cognito Forms, which processes and stores submissions on our behalf.

Please don’t include detailed medical information in our website forms. We’re happy to discuss your loved one’s health privately by phone or in person.

How we use it

  • To respond to your questions and schedule tours
  • To review job applications
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our website and forms, or when required by law.

Cookies and third-party services

Our website does not use advertising cookies. Some pages use third-party services that may set their own cookies: Cognito Forms on our form pages, and Google Maps if you choose to load the map on our contact page. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Security

Our website is served over a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS). No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us.

Children’s privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
